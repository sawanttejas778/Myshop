Enterprise ERP – User Help Manual

This guide explains every core process in the system. Use the sidebar to navigate through sections.

Getting Started

  • 1 Login / Signup: Use your registered email and password. If you don't have an account, click Sign Up – an OTP will be sent to your email for verification. Complete the OTP step to activate your account.
  • 2 Role & Permissions: Your role (Admin, Owner, or User) controls what you see. Admins manage shops and users; Users only have access to assigned shops.
  • 3 Forgot Password: On the login page, click Forgot Password. You will receive a reset link via email (valid for 1 hour). Follow the link to set a new password.
  • 4 First Login: Admins must select a shop from the top bar (or via the Shop switcher) to start managing inventory, orders, and customers for that specific shop.

Shop Management

  • 1 Create a New Shop: Go to Add Shop. Enter the Shop Name, GSTN, Address, Phone, and Tax ID. Only Admins/Owners can create shops.
  • 2 Switch Between Shops: Use the Shop Selector dropdown at the top of the dashboard. All data (products, customers, orders) will switch to the selected shop context.
  • 3 Dashboard KPIs: Once a shop is selected, the dashboard shows Total Sales (completed/delivered orders), Orders Today, Total Products, and Low Stock Items (where stock ≤ safe_stock).

Products

  • 1 Add Product: Navigate to Products → Add New. Fill in Name, Price, Buying Price (Bprice), Tax %, Stock, Safe Stock, Unit (e.g., KG, PC), HSN Code, and Location. Assign an existing Category or type a new one to create it on the fly.
  • 2 Upload Image: In the product edit screen, use the image uploader. The system saves images as product_{id}.jpg/png and automatically cleans up old images.
  • 3 Descriptions: You can add a multi-line description. The system splits it into 5 distinct description fields (Description1 to Description5) for structured product details.
  • 4 SPID Generation: The system auto-generates a unique SPID (Stock Keeping ID) based on the product name and timestamp, ensuring traceability.
  • 5 Bulk Import/Export: Admins can use the Import Products endpoint (JSON/API) to quickly add multiple products at once. Export products via Export to get a full JSON list.

Customers

  • 1 Create Customer: Go to Customer → Add New. Required fields: Name, Mobile, Address, City, Pincode, Village, and Email. Optional: GSTN, Bank IFSC, Bank Account, and Bank Name.
  • 2 Global vs Shop Linking: If a customer already exists in the system (by email), they are automatically linked to the current shop. If not, a new customer record is created and linked.
  • 3 Search: Use the Customer Search bar to find customers by Name, Mobile, Email, City, Village, or Pincode. Results are filtered by the selected shop.
  • 4 Customer Details: Clicking on a customer shows their full profile, transaction history, and linked quotations/invoices.

Suppliers

  • 1 Add Supplier: Navigate to Suppliers → Add New. Enter Name, Email, Phone, Address, Pincode, State, City, Country, GSTN, Bank details, and Payment Terms.
  • 2 Search Suppliers: The search bar filters suppliers by Name, Email, Phone, GSTN, City, or State within the current shop.
  • 3 Supplier Count: The dashboard or supplier list shows the total number of suppliers linked to your active shop.

Procure‑to‑Pay (PR/PO/GR)

The system supports a complete internal procurement cycle.

  • 1 Create Purchase Requisition (PR): Go to PR/PO → New PR. Select Supplier, add items (Product, Quantity, Unit Price, Tax Rate), and provide a Reason. The system calculates Subtotal, Tax, and Grand Total automatically.
  • 2 Convert PR to Purchase Order (PO): In the PR list, click Convert to PO on a pending PR. The system creates a PO with status pending. You can update the PO status later (Approved, Received, Cancelled).
  • 3 Print PO: Once a PO is created, use the Print PO button to generate a PDF/printable version. This marks the PO as printed (Print_status = Y), preventing further edits.
  • 4 Gate Receipt (GR) – Receive Goods: Go to Gate Receipt. Select a PO, enter the quantities actually received (partial receipts are allowed). The system checks the remaining PO quantity and validates against it.
  • 5 Automatic Stock Update: Upon creating a Gate Receipt, the system increases the product stock in the Products table. The PO's recieved_QTY is updated. If the full PO quantity is received, the PO is marked as completed.
  • 6 Update PR/PO: You can update PRs and POs as long as they haven't been printed or converted to a higher stage.

Sales & Orders

  • 1 Add to Cart: Browse products on the customer dashboard, and click Add to Cart. You can adjust quantities directly in the cart view.
  • 2 Checkout: Go to View Cart → Proceed to Checkout. The system calculates Subtotal, Tax (based on product tax rates), and Grand Total.
  • 3 Place Order: Enter shipping/contact details (Phone, Address, City, State, Zip, Country). Click Place Order. The system creates an Order with status pending, clears the cart, and inserts the order items & descriptions.
  • 4 Manage Orders (Admin): Admins see a list of all orders in Manage Orders. They can update the status (e.g., pending → delivered → cancelled).
    Important: Moving an order to delivered deducts stock. Moving it back to cancelled restores stock.
  • 5 Order History: Users can view their My Orders page, showing all past orders with dates and statuses.

Order Returns

  • 1 Eligibility: Only Delivered orders are eligible. The return window is 7 days from the delivery date.
  • 2 Request Return: In My Orders, click Return next to the eligible item. The system submits a return_requested status for that order.
  • 3 Return Status: You can view the return status, estimated refund amount, and return-by date on the Return Status page (accessible from the order history).

Quotations & Invoices

  • 1 Create Quotation: Go to Quotations → New Quotation. Select an existing Customer, add items with quantities and unit prices. The system calculates CGST/SGST/IGST and total.
  • 2 Convert to Invoice: In the quotations list, click Convert to Invoice. This creates an invoice, moves the quotation status to sent, and deducts the stock (since the goods are effectively sold).
  • 3 View Invoices: Admins can see all invoices in Invoices (filtered by shop). Customers can see their own invoices in My Invoices.
  • 4 Update Invoices: Invoices can be updated (e.g., change status to paid, edit items) as long as they are not locked. The system automatically handles stock adjustments if quantities change.
  • 5 Invoice Printing: The invoice page provides a print-friendly view for physical billing.

Khatabook (Ledger)

  • 1 Parties: Add parties (vendors or customers) with Name, Phone, Address, and Type (e.g., Supplier or Customer). Each party has a running Balance.
  • 2 Add Transaction: For a selected party, add a new transaction (Credit or Debit). Enter the Amount, Description, and Date. The balance updates automatically.
  • 3 View History: Click on any party to see the last 10 transactions in reverse chronological order. This gives a quick snapshot of outstanding dues.

Stock Adjustments

  • 1 Manual Adjustment: Go to Stock Adjustment. Select a Product, enter the Quantity (positive for increase, negative for decrease), and add a Reason. The system logs the adjustment in the stock_adjustment table and updates the product stock instantly.
  • 2 Why Use This? Use for cycle counts, damaged goods write-offs, or correcting data entry errors. All adjustments are auditable with user and timestamp.

Stock Transfers (Between Shops)

  • 1 Initiate Transfer: Go to Stock Transfer. Select Source Shop (where stock exists) and Destination Shop. Add products with quantities and optional location override.
  • 2 Validation: The system checks if the source shop has sufficient stock. If not, the transfer fails for that product.
  • 3 Automatic Product Creation: If the destination shop does NOT have this product, the system creates a new product in the destination shop, copying all attributes (Name, Price, Bprice, SPID, Category, HSN, Unit, Tax, etc.). The stock is set to the transferred quantity.
  • 4 Stock Update: The source shop's stock is decreased, and the destination shop's stock is increased. The transfer is logged in the stock_transfer table for auditing.
  • 5 Partial Transfers: You can transfer a portion of the available stock. The remaining stock stays in the source shop.

Reports & Settings

  • 1 Reports: The Reports section (accessible from the top nav) provides detailed insights into sales trends, product performance, and transaction logs. Data is filterable by shop and date range.
  • 2 Settings: Under Settings, users can update their profile, change passwords, and configure shop-specific preferences (e.g., default tax rates, currency, etc.).
  • 3 System Logs: All critical actions (orders, stock changes, adjustments) are logged. Admins can monitor system health and user activities via the logs directory (configured in app.py).

This help guide covers all primary workflows. For technical issues, check the application logs or contact your system administrator.
